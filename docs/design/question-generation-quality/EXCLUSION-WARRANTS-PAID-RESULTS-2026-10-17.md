## Next implementation priorities

1. Keep mechanical citation validity separate from semantic entailment. A cited
   passage does not establish an exclusive action or refute a weaker claim.
   Introduce explicit source-condition and claim-strength comparisons, with
   adversarial controls and an independent targeted review when necessary.
2. Represent planned alternatives versus jointly required operations explicitly,
   rather than allowing a reviewer to expand an English disjunction into a new
   mandatory task. Do not change the user's fixed request to obtain a pass.
3. Ground notation defects in a concrete incompatible source convention or
   failed value/unit equivalence. Extra clarity about consistent units alone
   must not be treated as a contradiction; closed-formula proofs remain a
   separate requirement.
4. Measure reviewer false refusals and unsafe passes separately from eligibility,
   using teacher labels before enabling this protocol by default. Any subsequent
   paid model comparison needs a new bounded declaration and authorization.
