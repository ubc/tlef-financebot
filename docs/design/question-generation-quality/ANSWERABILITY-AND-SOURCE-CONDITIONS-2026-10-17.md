## Why this follows the provenance work

The independently audited saved Drafts P01/P04 had exact relevant source quotes.
Their failure was semantic: mitigation was treated as elimination, and a listed
table strategy was treated as the exclusive strategy. One content reviewer even
waived uncertainty because it affected a distractor. A single `correct` role label
and a supported intended solution do not establish a uniquely answerable MCQ.

The ten-file outline separately retained 251 extracted conditions. Pack synthesis
has original quotes, and independent final grounding also sees full originals.
However, the final organizer receives summarized pack objectives without an
explicit condition catalog. Necessary qualifiers can disappear between stages.
L82/E482 and L138/E947 are retained diagnostic examples, not a new quality score.

## Implemented validation behavior

- New v2 durable requests select `option-answerability-v1` in server-owned
  authoring requirements. Existing exact retries and cached UUID responses keep
  their original schema, including historical absence.
- The existing source assessment call checks every literal MCQ response against
  the actual stem. Its structured rows distinguish fully correct, not fully
  correct and undetermined answers, with bounded source references and explicit
  exclusion grounds. A claim in corrective feedback is not the answer text.
- The server requires exact option-key coverage and one resolved answer matching
  the keyed option. Ambiguity or unresolved exclusion withholds the candidate;
  existing supported bounded repair can then propose a new candidate, which must
  independently pass all ordinary checks. No extra model call or retry loop is
  introduced by this assessment contract.
- True/False assesses the whole proposition once. The server maps its truth to
  the actual True/False option order. Numerical verification remains separate;
  source, memory, requirements and difficulty checks are not waived.
- Historical assessments are not reinterpreted as new-protocol passes. Prompt,
  schema, budget and output changes remain explicit in the next preflight.

## Implemented multi-chapter input repair

- Preserve extracted conditions as a separate immutable, versioned sidecar.
  Join only exact material/chunk/section/passage identities; labels and numerical
  similarity cannot attach a condition to a neighboring source point.
- Give each synthesis pack only its linked conditions and deduplicate original
  references. Give final organization bounded compact condition context with
  pinned original aliases. Conditions remain advisory extracted context; exact
  originals remain the authority for independent grounding.
- K identities are not learning points, LOs or mapped coverage. Orphans remain
  explicit. No silent top-k selection or truncation is permitted to meet budget.
  New requests select the context policy; absent historical requests preserve
  legacy rendering and exact checkpoint/cache behavior. Extraction caches keep
  their existing prompt/schema identities.
