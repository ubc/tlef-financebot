## Product implications

Keep supply counters separate: requested numerical tasks, optional conceptual alternatives, honest evidence gaps and schema errors. Present missing plotted values as a source gap with a visual-review/reupload action, rather than silently treating a conceptual fallback as numerical quota fulfillment. Preserve source and notation eligibility while separately checking whether a scenario adds an inference beyond Bank recall. Keep achieved difficulty visible when it differs from the request. These are recommendations; this audit changes no product behavior.
