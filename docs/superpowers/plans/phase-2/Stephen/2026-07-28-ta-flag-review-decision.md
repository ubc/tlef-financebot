# TA Flag Review — Deferred Product Decision

**Owner:** Stephen
**Status:** decision recorded; TA page implementation deferred

## Confirmed role boundary

- Instructors may edit a flagged question and update the real Question Bank
  directly.
- Instructors may return an unchanged question to students or reject and
  archive it.
- Instructors may add an internal teaching-team comment and a separate,
  optional student-facing reply.
- TAs must not approve, archive, publish, notify affected students, or directly
  complete an instructor flag resolution.
- A future TA page should allow a TA to inspect and edit a proposed revision,
  add an internal comment, and submit the proposal to the Instructor Review
  Queue.
- The instructor remains responsible for the final one-click approval or
  Reject & Archive action.

## Required future workflow

1. TA opens a flagged question in a TA-specific review context.
2. TA edits a proposed revision without publishing it to the live Question
   Bank.
3. TA optionally adds an internal review comment.
4. TA selects **Submit to Review Queue**.
5. Instructor reviews the proposed revision, can make further changes, and
   chooses the final action.

The current bug-fix work intentionally does not add a TA page or expand TA
permissions.
